Indore Places Gallery

Rajwada Palace

Rajwada Palace is one of the most famous historical landmarks in Indore. Built by the Holkars, it is known for its unique architecture and rich royal heritage.

Lal Bagh Palace

Lal Bagh Palace is a beautiful historic palace in Indore. It is known for its grand architecture, beautiful interiors, and royal heritage.

Gandhi Hall

Gandhi Hall is a famous historic building in Indore. It is known for its attractive architecture, large clock tower, and beautiful Indo-Gothic design.

Chappan Dukan

Chappan Dukan is one of the most popular food destinations in Indore. It offers a variety of snacks, sweets, street food, and local delicacies.

Sarafa Bazaar

Sarafa Bazaar is a famous night food market in Indore. It becomes lively in the evening and is popular for local street foods and sweets.

Khajrana Temple

Khajrana Ganesh Temple is a well-known religious place in Indore. It is dedicated to Lord Ganesha and is visited by many devotees throughout the year.

Annapurna Temple

Annapurna Temple is a beautiful temple in Indore dedicated to Goddess Annapurna. It is known for its colorful entrance,peaceful surroundings,and religious importance.

Regional Park

Regional Park is a popular recreational place in Indore. It has green spaces, a lake, walking areas, and beautiful surroundings for visitors to enjoy.

Pipliyapala Lake

Pipliyapala Lake is a scenic attraction in Indore surrounded by greenery. It is a popular place for boating, relaxing, and enjoying the peaceful environment.

Ralamandal

Ralamandal is a scenic hill area near Indore known for its greenery and natural surroundings. It is a popular destination for nature lovers and visitors looking for a peaceful outdoor experience.

Mandu

Mandu is a historic destination near Indore, famous for its beautiful forts, palaces, and ancient architecture. It is a popular place to explore Madhya Pradesh’s rich history.
